# Combined-galaxy / convergence premise

> **NON-BINDING WORKING PREMISE.** The cause, physics, timing, and story consequences are intentionally undefined.

## Core idea

OBP may take place in a navigable region where the former **Solana** and **Bogon** galactic spaces are no longer cleanly separated.

This allows worlds that originally lived on different sequel star maps to coexist on one OBP star map. For example, Endako and Kerwan can be reachable within the same navigation structure rather than requiring the player to choose a game/campaign boundary.

The useful observable fact is simply:

> At some point, Solana and Bogon ceased to be cleanly separated navigable regions.

Do **not** currently define whether this was a literal galactic collision, dimensional event, technology, natural phenomenon, villain action, or something else.

## Preserve origin identity

Physical/navigation convergence does not require cultural homogenisation.

- Kerwan remains historically/culturally a Solana world.
- Endako remains historically/culturally a Bogon world.
- Native architecture, ecology, governments, corporations, technologies, and history can remain recognisable.

A useful design principle:

> **The places still remember where they came from, even if the star map no longer does.**

## Why this helps OBP

The premise makes cross-game interaction feel like worldbuilding rather than imported bonus content.

Potential consequences:

- Gadgetron and Megacorp can enter direct competition.
- Thugs-4-Less can operate in former Solana space.
- Rangers can deploy into former Bogon worlds.
- Tyhrranoid/Nefarious threats can reach Bogon destinations.
- local species can migrate while retaining homeworld identities.
- arena, racing, ship, and trade circuits can span both former regions.
- old abandoned facilities may become strategically useful because routes changed.
- planets can gain new neighbours and political/economic relevance.

## Star-map presentation

The final map does not need to look perfectly uniform. It may be useful to retain recognisable historical clusters or visually suggest an overlap/interpenetration region.

Possible conceptual structure:

```text
former Solana cluster
      \
       \   overlap / mixed routes
        X
       /
      /
former Bogon cluster
```

This is only a presentation idea. Do not prescribe exact planet positions yet.

## World-state implications

The convergence can justify change without forcing a detailed plot immediately:

- old trade routes changed
- corporations expanded
- migration occurred
- criminal groups exploited new routes
- Rangers gained a larger operating area
- wildlife/ecologies can overlap selectively
- old military/industrial facilities gained new relevance

This supports OBP's broader desire for planets to have history and evolving states rather than acting as one-use levels.

## Explicitly unresolved

Do not decide yet:

- what caused the convergence
- when it occurred relative to original story beats
- whether it was instantaneous or gradual
- whether the original trilogy chronology is preserved
- whether every original story event still happens
- exact planet coordinates/routes
- whether the combined region has a new formal name
- whether characters understand the cause

Those decisions should remain open until the broader OBP campaign/story structure becomes clearer.

## Current wording for other brainstorming documents

When a short premise is needed, use something like:

> **Combined-galaxy premise:** OBP explores a navigable region in which former Solana and Bogon space has become physically interconnected or intermixed. Planets retain their historical identities and origins, while the cause and exact nature of the convergence remain deliberately undefined.
