## Native-derived behavior and currently hosted boundaries

The initialization branch *priority* at `FUN_L06_002e8678` is carrier selector (`state 1`), hidden/respawn selector (`state 15`), authored path entrance (`state 3`), then ordinary roaming (`state 8`). A path source proceeds through `state 4` path traversal, `state 5` airborne transition, `state 7` landing and returns to 8. State-8 roaming selects a home-relative destination within 0.5 to 2 native units and refreshes its destination after a 45–75 native-tick counter or arrival within 0.05 units. The recovered native movement is conditional on Moby byte `+0x31`; the host offers a bounded, deterministic local-PRNG substitute for admitted ordinary Frogs, with an explicit `roamingAllowed` gate where the native byte is not yet available. Roaming can enter 9 when target acquisition is enabled, with an original 1-in-9 selector; 9 chases the target and enters attack `state 12`, sequence 5, when closer than two native units. Frames 10–15 of that animation double the forward displacement and dispatch a radius-0.333, damage-1 common attack helper. Attack contact is eligible throughout the window and coalesced after a successful hit until the next loop. State 10 is alternate approach, state 11 its animation-gated jump, state 13 retreat, state 16 grabbed. Damage helper `0x2e94f0` removes health-depleted actors when the lives selector is zero; otherwise decrements lives, resets source HP, returns to spawn and parks in `state 15` for a separate respawn trigger.

## Source-backed hidden / respawn launch, and current admission boundary

The missing activation function is **`FUN_L06_002e9d10`**, recovered in Nebula G34's hangar-door overlay (also `func_L06_002EB140` in the independent disassembly). It only accepts a native class-827 source **already in state 15**. Two genuine producers invoke the same helper: `FUN_L06_002fda30` in the level-6 trigger actor first requires Ratchet in its PVar `+0x04` clip volume, waits for an animation signal, then attempts **one listed state-15 Moby every 20 native frames** until exhausted. ## Native Alien Queen damage recovery preceding Frog group22

The LEVEL6 original Queen (class 1051, placement 626, 0x1b0-byte authored PVar) has a separate **native damage and recovery state machine**, `FUN_L06_002fa9c0`, now captured by `Rac1AlienQueenRecovery`. On initialization, the original Queen has **30 health**. A qualifying hit is scaled by its provenance class: `0x131` contributes 0.666x damage, while `0x79`, `0xb0`, and class-827 `0x33b` contribute **1.5x**. Hits are not admitted during source recovery states 3 and 5, death state 14 or terminal state 15. With enough native countdown remaining, the first crossing of health **20** clamps to 20, protects the threshold during a **30-native-tick recovery**, then sets health to **19.9**, state **3**, animation **6**. The later crossing of **10** clamps to 10, similarly recovers 30 native ticks, and produces health **9.9**, state **5**, animation **6**. The first crossing takes precedence even if one hit would otherwise cross both thresholds. Native death chooses state14 and animation10 after the final health is exhausted. The boss source path/camera reset calls remain unhosted, and the underlying common weapon-contact solver is not approximated here.

The Godot integration now loads the *original Queen class-1051 retail animation asset*, retains its source placement and uses this bounded health/recovery adapter. `TickRac1QueenRecovery` owns the 30-tick countdown, selects **retail animation6** at the original state3 transition, and waits for that source sequence to finish before calling `Rac1FrogQueenGroup` to dispatch twelve source-owned Frog launches. A sequence-clock wrap is an acknowledged Godot equivalent for the original `moby+0x70 & 2` completion signal, not a frame-perfect source callback. The source then switches to Queen animation1 within state3; the separate native camera interpolator must complete before moving to state4. OBP **does not yet produce authenticated Queen hit records or the preceding native boss-entrance, airborne/navigation and camera conditions in ordinary player gameplay**. Those are important next integration tasks, not silently replaced with distance/timer guesses.
